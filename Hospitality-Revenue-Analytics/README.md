# Hospitality Revenue Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Hospitality Revenue Analytics Dashboard is an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed in Power BI to help hospitality businesses monitor revenue performance, customer behavior, staff productivity, and sales effectiveness through interactive reporting and KPI-driven insights.

The project demonstrates how transactional business data can be transformed into actionable recommendations that support operational and strategic decision-making.


## Business Objectives

- Analyze revenue performance and sales trends
- Measure customer retention and loyalty behavior
- Evaluate staff productivity and performance
- Assess the impact of upselling initiatives
- Identify high-performing products and categories
- Support data-driven decision-making through KPI reporting


## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel


## Data Model

The dashboard was built using a Star Schema data model to ensure efficient reporting and scalable analytics.

### Fact Table
- Sales_Transactions

### Dimension Tables
- Customer_Data
- Staff_Performance
- Date_Table

This structure supports optimized filtering, KPI calculations, and business reporting.


## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Customer Retention Rate
- Upsell Revenue
- Product Performance
- Staff Productivity


## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Revenue Analytics
Provides an executive overview of revenue performance, sales trends, peak operating hours, category contribution, and payment method analysis.

### 2. Product & Sales Analytics
Analyzes best-selling products, category performance, discount impact, and sales contribution across menu items.

### 3. Staff Productivity
Evaluates staff performance, revenue contribution, customer ratings, upselling effectiveness, and operational productivity.

### 4. Customer Retention Analytics
Examines customer loyalty, retention behavior, repeat customer trends, and customer segmentation.

### 5. Executive Summary
Consolidates key findings, business recommendations, and strategic insights derived from the analysis.


## Key Insights

- Total revenue exceeded AED 363K across the reporting period.
- Main Course products generated the highest revenue contribution.
- Customer retention remained above 96%, indicating strong repeat business.
- Upselling initiatives positively influenced Average Order Value (AOV).
- Revenue activity peaked during lunch and evening operating periods.
- High-performing staff consistently exceeded average sales performance.


## Business Recommendations

- Increase staffing during peak demand periods to improve service efficiency.
- Expand upselling initiatives through targeted staff training.
- Prioritize promotion of high-performing menu categories.
- Strengthen customer loyalty programs to encourage repeat visits.
- Replicate best practices from top-performing staff members.
- Continue leveraging data-driven reporting to support strategic decision-making.
