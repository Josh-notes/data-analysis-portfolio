# Hospitality Revenue Analytics

A Power BI hospitality analytics project focused on revenue, customer retention, product sales, staff productivity, and operational performance.

The project brings several areas of hospitality performance into one reporting environment so that commercial and operational patterns can be reviewed together.

## Executive Dashboard

![Hospitality Revenue Analytics Executive Summary](Executive%20Summary.png)

## Business Questions

The project was structured around practical hospitality questions:

* How is revenue performing across the reporting period?
* Which products contribute most to sales?
* What does customer retention look like?
* How does staff performance vary?
* Where are the strongest commercial and operational patterns?
* How can management reporting bring these areas together in one view?

## Analytical Approach

The project follows a business focused reporting workflow:

**Business Questions → Data Preparation → Data Model → Analysis → Dashboard Pages → Management Insight**

Power Query was used for data preparation, with Power BI and DAX supporting the analytical model, measures, KPIs, and dashboard reporting.

The project is organised around multiple analytical views rather than relying on a single summary page.

## Business Perspective

Hospitality performance cannot be understood through revenue alone.

Sales, customers, products, and staff performance interact with one another. Looking at these areas together provides more context than reviewing isolated KPIs.

For example, a change in revenue may deserve further investigation through product performance, customer activity, or staff contribution before management decides what action to take.

The purpose of this project is therefore not simply to display KPIs. It is to organise operational information in a way that makes those relationships easier to explore.

## Business Use

A reporting solution of this type can support management conversations around:

* Revenue performance
* Product sales
* Customer retention
* Staff productivity
* Operational performance
* Management reporting
* Areas requiring deeper investigation

The dashboards provide analytical evidence for these conversations. They do not claim that recommendations were implemented in a live hospitality business or that specific financial improvements resulted from the project.
