## How I Approach Analytics

**Understand the business question → prepare and validate the data → analyse the relevant drivers → communicate what the evidence supports → identify where deeper investigation is needed**

I prefer analysis that is reproducible, commercially relevant, and defensible in an interview or stakeholder discussion.

Where a portfolio project demonstrates analytical potential rather than a live implementation, I state that distinction clearly.

---

## Portfolio Principles

Every featured project is documented around the work that can actually be inspected in the repository.

I do not present projected improvements as realised business outcomes, exploratory indicators as confirmed results, or portfolio case studies as live client deployments.

The goal is simple: **credible analysis, clear communication, and useful business thinking.**
